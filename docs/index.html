01 · Research Question

Can the crowd tell us what happens next?

StockTwits gives investors a place to discuss individual stocks and label their own posts as bullish or bearish. We wanted to know whether that collective sentiment contains information about future market movement — or whether it is mostly noise.

Can StockTwits sentiment help predict the future direction of U.S. stocks?

02 · The Data

Millions of opinions. One question.

We combine investor sentiment with historical market data, align observations by ticker and trading date, and study whether today's activity tells us anything useful about tomorrow.

03 · Method

Prediction and pattern discovery

01

Build daily market and sentiment features

Investor activity is summarized by ticker and day, then aligned with price movement, trading volume, volatility, and related market features.

02

Predict next-day direction

Logistic Regression and Random Forest models test whether market and sentiment features can distinguish future up-days from down-days.

03

Discover behavioral regimes

K-Means clustering groups ticker-days with similar sentiment, attention, and same-day market behavior. Next-day returns are held out so they can be evaluated after the clusters are formed.

06 · Why It Matters

A prediction tool can influence the market it observes.

For investors

Social sentiment can feel like real-time intelligence, but apparent confidence online does not necessarily translate into reliable predictive information.

For responsible AI

Financial models can amplify herd behavior, sampling bias, and false certainty. Baselines, time-aware validation, and careful leakage controls matter just as much as model complexity.
